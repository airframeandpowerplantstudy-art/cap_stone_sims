Decimals

Decimals are another way to show parts of a whole. Fractions and decimals can represent the same value, but decimals are written using a decimal point.

This matters in real life because technicians use decimals when working with measurements, calipers, micrometers, gauges, tolerances, fluid amounts, and machine settings. A measurement may be written as 0.25 inch, 1.5 gallons, or 2.375 inches.

Decimals are not just math class stuff. They are measurement stuff.

 

1. Decimal Place Value :

Decimal place value tells us what each digit means after the decimal point. The farther right the digit goes, the smaller the value becomes.

Decimal Place Value Fraction Meaning How to Say It
0.1 Tenths 1/10 one tenth
0.01 Hundredths 1/100 one hundredth
0.001 Thousandths 1/1000 one thousandth

Example: In the number 124.673, the 6 is in the tenths place, the 7 is in the hundredths place, and the 3 is in the thousandths place.

 

2. Rounding Decimals :

Rounding decimals means shortening a number to a certain place value. This is useful when a measurement is more precise than needed.

The rule is simple: look at the digit to the right of the place you are rounding to. If it is 5 or higher, round up. If it is 4 or lower, keep the number the same.

Original Number Round To Rounded Number
212.765957 Thousandths 212.766
212.765957 Hundredths 212.77
212.765957 Tenths 212.8
212.765957 Whole Number 213

 

3. Adding Decimals :

When adding decimals, line up the decimal points. This keeps the place values lined up correctly.

Example: 4.625 + 3.8 = ?

Step Work
Line up decimals    4.625
+ 3.800
Add 8.425

Final answer: 8.425

 

4. Subtracting Decimals :

Subtracting decimals works the same way. Line up the decimal points. If needed, add zeros to hold the place value.

Example: 53.59 - 8.632 = ?

Step Work
Add a zero 53.590
- 8.632
Subtract 44.958

Final answer: 44.958

 

5. Multiplying Decimals :

When multiplying decimals, multiply like whole numbers first. Then count the total number of decimal places in the problem and place the decimal in the answer.

Example: 7.32 x 14 = ?

Step Work
Ignore decimal first 732 x 14 = 10248
Count decimal places 7.32 has 2 decimal places
14 has 0 decimal places
Total = 2 decimal places
Place decimal 102.48

Final answer: 102.48

 

6. Dividing Decimals :

When dividing decimals, the decimal point depends on where the decimal is located in the problem.

Decimal in the Dividend

If the decimal is only in the dividend, bring the decimal straight up into the answer.

Example: 12.6 ÷ 3 = ?

Problem Answer
12.6 ÷ 3 4.2

Decimal in the Divisor

If the divisor has a decimal, move the decimal to the right until the divisor becomes a whole number. Then move the decimal in the dividend the same number of places.

Example: 4.5 ÷ 0.5 = ?

Step Work
Move decimals 4.5 ÷ 0.5 becomes 45 ÷ 5
Divide 45 ÷ 5 = 9

Final answer: 9

 

7. Converting Between Decimals and Fractions :

Fractions to Decimals

A fraction is another way to write a division problem. To change a fraction into a decimal, divide the numerator by the denominator.

Example: 1/8 = ?

Fraction Divide Decimal
1/8 1 ÷ 8 0.125

Decimals to Fractions

To change a decimal into a fraction, say the decimal in words and write it as a fraction. Then reduce it if possible.

Example: 0.125

Step Work
Say it one hundred twenty-five thousandths
Write it 125/1000
Reduce it 1/8

To The Nearest Fraction

You may have to do this when measuring when your math doesn't come out to an exact fractional decimal. You need to get close enough with your measuring tools to what you calculated.

To change a decimal into the nearest fractional equivalent, you take the decimal and multiply by the desired denominator to get the numerator. 

Example: 0.127 to the nearest 16th of an inch.

Step Work
Multiply it .127 x 16 =2.032
Round it 2
Put it up 2/16

8. Converting Between Decimals and Percentages :

Percent means out of 100. Decimals and percentages are closely connected.

Decimal to Percentage

To convert a decimal to a percentage, move the decimal point two places to the right and add a percent sign or multiply by 100. 

Decimal Move Decimal Percentage
0.50 two places right 50%

Percentage to Decimal

To convert a percentage to a decimal, move the decimal point two places to the left and remove the percent sign or divide by 100.

Percentage Move Decimal Decimal
75% two places left 0.75

Real-World Decimal Practice

Use these as aviation, automotive, robotics, welding, or manufacturing warmups.

Problem 1:   Round 212.765957 to the nearest hundredth.

Reveal Answer & Steps
Correct Answer: 212.77

Step-by-step breakdown:
1. The hundredths place is the second digit after the decimal.
2. In 212.765957, the hundredths digit is 6.
3. Look to the next digit: 5.
4. Since 5 or higher rounds up, 212.765957 becomes 212.77.

Problem 2:   Add 4.625 + 3.8.

Reveal Answer & Steps
Correct Answer: 8.425

Step-by-step breakdown:
1. Line up the decimal points.
2. Add a zero to 3.8 to make 3.800.
3. Add: 4.625 + 3.800 = 8.425.

Problem 3:   Subtract 53.59 - 8.632.

Reveal Answer & Steps
Correct Answer: 44.958

Step-by-step breakdown:
1. Line up the decimal points.
2. Add a zero to 53.59 to make 53.590.
3. Subtract: 53.590 - 8.632 = 44.958.

Problem 4:   Multiply 7.32 x 14.

Reveal Answer & Steps
Correct Answer: 102.48

Step-by-step breakdown:
1. Ignore the decimal first: 732 x 14 = 10248.
2. 7.32 has 2 decimal places.
3. Put 2 decimal places in the answer: 102.48.

Problem 5:   Convert 0.125 to a fraction.

Reveal Answer & Steps
Correct Answer: 1/8

Step-by-step breakdown:
1. Say 0.125 as one hundred twenty-five thousandths.
2. Write it as 125/1000.
3. Reduce 125/1000 to 1/8.

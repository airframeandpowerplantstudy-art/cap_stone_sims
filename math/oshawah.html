What counts as Working at Heights?

Treat work at height as any task where a person could fall to a lower level or where a falling object could strike someone below.

General Industry

≥ 4 ft is the threshold.

Construction

≥ 6 ft is the threshold.

Instructor note: site safety can treat any distance as Working at Heights when the risk is significant or obvious.

Who makes the rules?

OSHA sets the legal requirements for work conducted at heights.

ANSI, ASSE/ASSP, manufacturers, and employers may publish standards or procedures that are more specific or more restrictive than OSHA minimum requirements.

Teaching point: Always follow the most protective applicable rule: law, employer policy, site plan, manufacturer manual, or task-specific procedure.
